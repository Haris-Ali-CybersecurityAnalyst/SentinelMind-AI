# SentinelMind-AI
SentinelMind AI Security Operating System of the Future, SentinelMind AI continuously learns what's normal for YOUR organization, models a living security score in real time, and gives your team an AI officer who can explain exactly why that score changed - and what to do about it.
CyberState™
Most security tools ask 'does this match a rule?' CyberState asks a different question: what's normal for THIS organization, what just changed, and where is it heading?

CyberState continuously scores your organization across Identity, Endpoint, and Behavioral dimensions - learning your own baseline from real telemetry, not a generic industry rule. When something deviates, it doesn't just flag it - it explains exactly which factor moved the score and by how much.
Prediction Center™
Prediction Center projects where your CyberState score is heading over the next 7, 30, and 90 days using statistical trend analysis on your organization's own history.

We're direct about what this is: a statistical trend forecast, not a trained AI prediction. As more daily history accumulates, forecasts get more precise - and we'll tell you exactly how much history backs any given forecast, so you always know how much to trust it.

7 / 30 / 90-day trend projections per security dimension
Transparent confidence levels based on real data volume
Honest by design - never a number without a reason behind it
